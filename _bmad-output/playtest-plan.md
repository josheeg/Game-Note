# Playtest Plan: The Living Walk

**Version**: 1.0
**Created**: 2026-09-23
**Session Date(s)**: 2026-09-23 through 2026-09-30

---

## Overview

### Objective

Validate that players can navigate and comprehend The Living Walk's audio-first design without spoken language or text, achieving the design metrics for audio comprehension, emotional response, and wayfinding. Specifically:

1. Verify ≥75% of demo players reach the miniature payoff (hilltop finale comprehension)
2. Verify ≥60% of playtest feedback mentions "calm/beautiful/moved" unprompted
3. Verify playtesters find all seeded POIs by ear alone in < 2 min per area (wayfinding gate)
4. Test Listen comprehension, pacing, and emotional response for the audio-first cozy experience
5. Confirm the 20-40 min walk duration is sustainable and enjoyable

### Build Information

- **Version**: 1.0 (prototype build)
- **Platform**: PC (Steam demo / Web demo)
- **Content**: The Living Walk prototype with 3 biomes (Meadow, Oak Grove, Stream Crossing) — sufficient for core loop validation within 20-40 min
- **Known Issues**: None — build is stable for playtest session

### Success Criteria

 mapped to GDD design metrics:

| Metric | Target | Measured By |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| ≥75% reach miniature payoff | 75% of players reach hilltop finale | Post-session survey: "Did you understand the hilltop reprise?" |
| ≥60% mention "calm/beautiful/moved" unprompted | 60%+ unprompted emotional descriptors | Post-session open-feedback question |
| <2 min find-time per POI | <2 min per area by ear alone | Timed observation: time from area entry to POI identification by sound |
| ≥5,000 wishlists (launch) | Launch metric — tracked separately | Steam wishlist counter |
| ≥80% positive reviews (launch) | Launch metric — tracked separately | Review aggregation post-launch |
| ≥10% demo-to-purchase (launch) | Launch metric — tracked separately | Purchase conversion tracking |

## Participant Criteria

### Target Profile

- **Player Type**: Casual / cozy-game fans; walking-sim enthusiasts; audio-novelty curious
- **Genre Experience**: Familiar with atmospheric walking sims (A Short Hike, Gris, Proteus) or short decompress sessions
- **Age Range**: 25-45 (primary target demographic)
- **Other Criteria**: Must appreciate mood, sound, and emotional payoff over challenge; no aversion to wordless experiences; must use headphones or good stereo speakers (critical for audio-first design)

### Recruitment

- **Count**: 5 participants (minimum for pattern identification; aligns with workflow default)
- **Source**: Internal team contacts, community forum, social media — screened for appreciation of atmospheric/audio experiences
- **Compensation**: $20 gift card or equivalent per participant

### Exclusion Criteria

- Players who require constant gameplay challenge/competition
- Players who dislike atmospheric/meditative experiences
- Players who cannot use headphones or stereo audio (critical for Listen mechanic)

## Session Structure

**Total Duration**: ~40-60 minutes (20-40 min walk + pre/post-session)

### Pre-Session (10 minutes)

1. **Welcome & Context (3 min)**
   - Introduce yourself and the research purpose
   - Explain: "We're testing the game, not you — your feedback will shape the experience"
   - Emphasize: This is a wordless, audio-first experience — no spoilers will be shared

2. **Consent & Setup (4 min)**
   - Recording consent (audio + video of screen/ gameplay — optional but preferred)
   - Collect demographic info (age, gaming background, headphone use)
   - Input preferences: keyboard/mouse or gamepad
   - Any accessibility needs (colorblind palette, volume adjustments)

3. **Instructions (3 min)**
   - "Play as you normally would — there are no wrong answers"
   - "If comfortable, think out loud about what you're hearing and what you think it means"
   - "You can stop at any time — there are no fail states"
   - "The world speaks through sound — pay attention to what the audio is telling you"

### Gameplay Session (20-40 minutes)

**The Living Walk prototype walk** — participants experience a continuous walk through 3 biomes:

1. **The Meadow** (tutorial biome) — teaches Walk + Listen + first Locate puzzle; sparse score (~2 voices)
2. **The Oak Grove** — first Sequence puzzle (2 sound sources); introduces plant-shaping (first gate)
3. **The Stream Crossing** — first Awaken puzzle; growth-bridge terrain shaping

**Focus Areas During Gameplay**:

- **Listen comprehension**: Can players identify what different sound sources mean? (chime = guidance, creature motif = hidden fragment, drone = approaching hush)
- **Pacing**: Does the 20-40 min walk feel appropriately paced? Too short? Too long?
- **Wayfinding**: Can players find points of interest by ear alone in < 2 min per area?
- **Emotional response**: What emotions arise naturally? (curiosity, wonder, calm, moved feeling)
- **Puzzle comprehension**: Do audio puzzles (Locate/Sequence/Awaken) communicate their goals without text?

**Intervention Rules**:

- Let players struggle (within reason) — the game has no fail states
- Note when you want to help — only intervene for:
  - Critical bugs preventing progress
  - Genuine distress
  - Session time running out
  - Player explicitly asks for help

### Post-Session (10-15 minutes)

1. **Immediate Reactions (3 min)**
   - "What was your overall impression of the experience?"
   - "What moment stood out the most?"
   - "Would you play again? Why or why not?"

2. **Structured Questions (5 min)**
   - "What did you understand the world was trying to tell you through sound?"
   - "Which sounds were most meaningful to you, and why?"
   - "Did you feel the world 'listened back' as you played? How so?"
   - "At any point were you confused about what to do or where to go? What sounded unclear?"

3. **Open Feedback (2 min)**
   - "Is there anything you wish the game had, sound-wise?"
   - "Anything else you'd like to share?"
   - "Questions for us?"

## Observation Guide

### Signals to Watch For

| Signal | Examples | Record |
|--------|----------|--------|
| **Confusion** | Pausing, circling, re-listening, looking around unsure | Location, duration, what sound source caused confusion |
| **Frustration** | Sighing, repeated attempts, gentle complaints | Cause, frequency, which mechanic |
| **Engagement** | Leaning in, "hmm" sounds, exclamations ("oh!", "nice"), continuing attentively | Features that work, moments of focus |
| **Boredom** | Checking phone, rushing, disengaging, yawning | Drop-off points, which biome/section |
| **Discovery** | "Oh!", trying things, audible reactions | What triggered the discovery (which sound/motif) |
| **Emotional Response** | Sighs of contentment, stillness, leaning back, quiet laughter | Which moment, qualitative description |

### Metrics to Collect

- Time to complete each biome area
- Time from area entry to POI identification (Listen comprehension)
- Number of Listen activations per area
- Success rate: can players find all seeded POIs by ear alone in < 2 min per area?
- Emotional response descriptors (from post-session survey)
- Think-aloud commentary transcribed verbatim
- Session duration (actual vs. expected 20-40 min)
- Narrative comprehension: "What was the walk about? What was the point?"

## Post-Playtest Analysis Framework

### Synthesize Findings

1. **Pattern Identification**
   - What issues appeared multiple times across participants?
   - What worked consistently well (sounds that communicated effectively, moments of clarity)?
   - Which biomes/areas had the highest confusion/engagement rates?

2. **Severity Assessment**
   - **Critical**: Blocks progression or makes core mechanics unplayable (e.g., players cannot comprehend Listen mechanic)
   - **Major**: Significantly impacts experience (e.g., pacing too slow, emotional goals not met)
   - **Minor**: Noticeable but manageable (e.g., one confusing sound source, minor pacing tweak)

3. **Recommendations**
   - Immediate fixes (audio cues to redesign, pacing adjustments)
   - Design considerations (which mechanics need clearer sonic signaling, which biomes need rebalancing)
   - Further investigation needed (A/B testing different sound designs, more participants)

## Notes

This playtest plan validates The Living Walk's core audio-first design pillars:

1. **Hearing is the sense** — Can players navigate and comprehend the world through sound alone?
2. **The world composes with you** — Do players understand their actions (planting, movement) affect the generative score?
3. **Gentle, deep world-shaping** — Do players feel their plantings have permanent impact?
4. **One walk, one payoff** — Do players reach the hilltop finale with comprehension of the full journey's song?

The design metrics (≥75% payoff reach, ≥60% "calm/beautiful/moved" feedback, <2 min POI find-time) are the primary validation targets. Secondary goals include confirming the 20-40 min walk duration and the audio-first coziness goals.
